## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and the name you
provide. If you register with an email and password, your password is stored only as a salted hash
by our authentication provider — we never see or store your password in plain text.

**Google account information.** If you sign in with Google, Google shares a limited profile with us:
your email address, your name, and your Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google
password, and we do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service.

**Study content.** We store the content you create in Phormula: flashcards, flashcard sets, sections,
folders, titles, descriptions, and any images you add to cards. We also store text you paste or
upload for flashcard generation. See Section 5 for how this content is processed.

**Waitlist information.** If you join our waitlist before launch, we collect your email address and
the date you signed up.

**Support correspondence.** If you email us, we keep your message, your email address, and our reply
so we can respond and maintain a support history.

### 2.2 Information created when you use Phormula

**Study activity.** We record activity needed to make the Service work: which sets you have created
or modified, card-level study state (such as whether a card is marked learned or bookmarked), and
timestamps for creation and updates.

**Email delivery records.** When we send you a transactional email, we log the message identifier,
the template used, the recipient address, and the delivery outcome. We keep a suppression list of
addresses that have unsubscribed or that have bounced, so that we do not email them again.

**Technical and security data.** Our hosting and infrastructure providers process standard server
logs on our behalf, which may include IP address, browser and device type, and request timestamps.
These are used for security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing faults.

**Bot-protection signals.** Our waitlist form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyses signals from
your browser to distinguish humans from automated traffic. Turnstile is designed to be
privacy-preserving and does not track you across websites.

### 2.3 What we deliberately do not collect

We think it is as important to state what we do not do:

- **We do not use analytics or tracking software.** Phormula contains no Google Analytics, no
  Meta Pixel, no PostHog, Mixpanel, Segment, Amplitude, Hotjar, or comparable product-analytics or
  session-recording tools.
- **We do not run advertising**, and we do not build advertising or behavioural profiles about you.
- **We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural
  advertising.
- **We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.**
- **We do not upload your source documents.** When you generate flashcards from a PDF or spreadsheet,
  the file is parsed entirely inside your own browser. Only the extracted text is sent to our
  servers. The original file never leaves your device.

## 5. AI Features and Your Study Material

Phormula uses artificial intelligence to generate flashcards from material you provide and to detect
text in images you upload. We want to be precise about what this involves.

**What is sent.** When you generate flashcards, the text you supply is sent to a third-party AI model
provider through an AI gateway operated by Lovable. When you use text detection on an image, the
image is sent to the same gateway. We currently route flashcard generation to **OpenAI** models and
text detection to **Google** models. These providers may change as we improve the Service; we will
keep this section current.

**What is not sent.** We do not send your name, your email address, your account identifier, or any
other account information along with your study material. The AI providers receive the content and
the instructions needed to process it — nothing that identifies you.

**We do not train AI models on your content.** We do not use your study material, your flashcards, or
anything else you create in Phormula to train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial intelligence
model, whether ours or a third party's. Our requests to AI providers are made under terms that
prohibit the provider from using submitted content for model training. This is a commitment, not a
default setting, and we will not change it without giving you clear advance notice and a meaningful
choice.

**Accuracy.** AI-generated flashcards can contain errors, omissions, or misinterpretations. Please
review generated content before relying on it for study or assessment. See our Terms of Service for
the corresponding disclaimer.

## 7. Cookies and Local Storage

Phormula uses only what it needs to function. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

- **Authentication tokens** are stored in your browser so you stay signed in between visits. Clearing
  them signs you out.
- **Interface preferences**, such as your sidebar state, are stored in a cookie.
- **Study and notification preferences** are stored in your browser's local storage under the keys
  `studyPrefs` and `notificationPrefs`, so your settings persist on that device.
- **A short-lived redirect marker** is stored in session storage during sign-in so we can return you
  to the page you originally requested. It is deleted immediately after use.

Because these are strictly necessary or purely functional, we do not display a consent banner for
them. You can clear this data through your browser settings, though doing so will sign you out and
reset your preferences.
